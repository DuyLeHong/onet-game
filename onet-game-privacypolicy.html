This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Onet Connect Animal: Tile Zoo (“the app”), published on Google Play by the developer FAVAS Group (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it.

In short

  • No accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and IP address.
  • We do not sell personal data.

1Information we collect

The app has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location. Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, boosters and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

2Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location);
  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with ads and the app (app launches, taps, video views);
  • diagnostic information (app performance and crash logs).

For this purpose the app uses the INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID permissions. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3Consent and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

4How information is used and shared

We do not sell personal data. We do not receive or store the data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK; it is shared with Google only for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as described above.

5Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (TLS).

6Retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you erase it in Settings › Reset progress or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policies. For any question or request about your data, email support@hdpsolutions.com.

7Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated up to PG).

8Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.

2विज्ञापन (Google AdMob)

ऐप मुफ़्त है और Google AdMob द्वारा दिए गए विज्ञापन दिखाता है। विज्ञापन दिखाने, उनका मापन करने और धोखाधड़ी रोकने के लिए Google Mobile Ads SDK अपने-आप निम्न जानकारी एकत्र करके Google के साथ साझा कर सकता है:

  • IP पता (अनुमानित लोकेशन का अंदाज़ा लगाने के लिए);
  • डिवाइस पहचानकर्ता, जैसे Android विज्ञापन आईडी और ऐप सेट आईडी (app set ID);
  • विज्ञापनों और ऐप के साथ इंटरैक्शन (ऐप खोलना, टैप, वीडियो देखना);
  • डायग्नोस्टिक जानकारी (ऐप का प्रदर्शन और क्रैश लॉग)।

इसके लिए ऐप INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE और AD_ID अनुमतियों का उपयोग करता है। Google इस डेटा को Google की निजता नीति (policies.google.com/privacy) के अनुसार प्रोसेस करता है। अधिक जानें: Google अपनी सेवाओं का उपयोग करने वाली साइटों या ऐप्स की जानकारी का उपयोग कैसे करता है।
