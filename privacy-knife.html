Kunai Dojo: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What the game stores

Kunai Dojo saves your progress, settings and the birth year you enter on first launch only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email or contacts. There is no chat and no way to share personal information inside the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data.

  • Players under 13 (from the birth year question): ad requests are marked as child-directed, only non-personalized ads rated G are requested, and we never ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).
  • Players 13 and over: in regions that require it, we ask for consent with Google's consent form first. On iOS we then ask for tracking permission. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy options or in your device settings. Ads are limited to PG content.

Game Center / Google Play Games

If you sign in, your score in the Dojo Challenge is sent to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games so it can appear on the leaderboard under your gaming nickname. Signing in is optional.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.
