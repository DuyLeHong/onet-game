Privacy Policy

Photo Background Changer (iOS) & Remove BG: Background Eraser (Android)
Developer: HDP Solution · Effective date: November 24, 2026

Summary

  • Background removal runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or to any third-party server.
  • We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and ad-related data, as described below.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile app published as “Photo Background Changer” on the Apple App Store and “Remove BG: Background Eraser” on Google Play (together, the “App”), developed by HDP Solution (“we”, “us”, “our”). It explains what information the App handles and why.

2. Your photos

  • On-device processing. When you remove or change a background, the App uses on-device machine-learning models (Apple Vision and a built-in U²-Net model on iOS; a built-in on-device model on Android). The photo is processed locally and never leaves your device.
  • Camera. The App uses the camera only when you choose to take a new photo to edit.
  • Photo library. The App opens only the photos you pick, and saves your edited photos and transparent PNG cut-outs to your photo library when you tap Save.
  • History. Recent edits may be kept inside the App's private storage on your device so you can reopen them. You can delete them in the App, and they are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Sharing. If you share an image, it goes only to the app or service you choose through your system's share sheet.

We do not have access to your photos, and we do not use them to train any model.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require sign-up or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts or precise location.

  • We do not offer in-app purchases, so we process no payment information.

5. Local settings

The App stores simple preferences on your device (for example, your last-used tools or whether you have seen the intro). This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your photos and settings are stored only on your device, you control them: delete items in the App, delete saved images from your photo library, or uninstall the App to remove all App data. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies; you can reset your advertising identifier at any time as described above.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security

Processing your photos on your device keeps them out of transit and off remote servers. Data handled by the advertising SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Your rights
